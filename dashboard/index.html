How does Gate 1 know the rover is arriving?

Gate 1's HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor fires 40 kHz sound pulses. When the echo bounces back from less than 5 cm away, it adds +1 to the Car Count and cycles Red → Yellow → Green!
